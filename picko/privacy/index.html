Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

This policy explains how Picko handles information when you use its coin flip, dice roll, card draw, and lucky wheel features.

Game activity

Picko generates random results on your device. You do not need to create an account to use the games. Picko does not operate a server that receives your game choices or results.

Advertising

The free version uses Google AdMob to display a banner on the home screen and occasional interstitial ads. Google and its partners may process information such as your IP address (which can indicate an approximate location), device or advertising identifiers where available, ad views and interactions, app interactions, and crash or performance information. This information may be used to deliver ads, measure their performance, prevent fraud, and maintain the advertising service. The data used depends on your device, region, and privacy choices.

Where required, Picko presents Google's consent form before requesting ads and provides a “Privacy options” control in the app so you can review or change your choice. Picko does not request Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission or access the advertising identifier (IDFA). Google explains its practices in its Privacy Policy and partner sites information.

Remove Ads purchase

Picko offers a one-time in-app purchase to remove ads. Apple processes the transaction through the App Store. Picko checks the purchase entitlement through StoreKit to determine whether ads should be shown; Picko does not receive your payment card details. After a valid purchase is recognized, Picko does not request or display AdMob ads. You can use “Restore Purchases” in the purchase screen. See Apple's Privacy Policy for Apple's handling of purchase information.

Support and your choices

If you email us for support, we receive the address and information you choose to include so we can respond. Do not send sensitive information unnecessarily. You can contact us about your support correspondence or privacy questions at haven.he@gmail.com. You can also manage ad privacy choices through the in-app control when it is available, and use your device and Apple account settings for other applicable controls.

Changes

We may update this page when Picko or its service providers change. The date above shows the latest revision.

隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 10 月 9 日

本政策说明你使用 Picko 的抛硬币、掷骰子、抽扑克牌和幸运转盘功能时，相关信息如何被处理。

游戏活动

Picko 在你的设备上生成随机结果。使用这些游戏无需创建账户。Picko 不运营接收游戏选择或结果的服务器。

广告

免费版本使用 Google AdMob，在首页展示横幅广告，并偶尔展示插页式广告。Google 及其合作伙伴可能处理 IP 地址（可用于推断大致位置）、设备或广告标识符（如可用）、广告展示与互动、App 交互，以及崩溃和性能信息。这些信息可能用于投放广告、衡量广告效果、防止欺诈及维护广告服务。实际使用的数据取决于设备、所在地区和隐私选择。

在适用地区，Picko 会在请求广告前展示 Google 同意表单，并在 App 内提供“隐私选项”，供你查看或更改选择。Picko 不请求 Apple 的 App 跟踪透明度权限，也不读取广告标识符 IDFA。详情请参阅 Google 的隐私权政策及合作伙伴网站信息。

永久去广告内购

Picko 提供一次性去广告内购，由 Apple 通过 App Store 处理付款。Picko 使用 StoreKit 核验购买权益，以决定是否展示广告；Picko 不会获得你的银行卡信息。确认有效购买后，Picko 不会请求或展示 AdMob 广告。你可以在购买页面使用“恢复购买”。Apple 如何处理购买信息，请参阅 Apple 隐私政策。

支持与选择

如果你通过电子邮件寻求支持，我们会收到你的邮箱地址以及你主动提供的信息，以便回复。请勿发送不必要的敏感信息。如需询问支持邮件或隐私问题，请联系 haven.he@gmail.com。在 App 提供“隐私选项”时，你也可以通过该入口管理广告隐私选择；其他适用控制可在设备及 Apple 账户设置中管理。

政策更新

当 Picko 或服务提供方发生变化时，我们可能更新此页面。页面顶部的日期为最近更新日期。
